## UAF Answer

Mathematics is not a separate Platonic world.
Mathematics is L-1: the structural substrate
of compressible invariance.

    M applies to D ⟺ K(D|M) ≪ K(D)

A mathematical structure is useful when it compresses.

### Wigner Problem

Mathematics works in physics because
mathematical structures are exactly
the patterns that survive compression.
No Platonic world needed. No multiverse needed.

### Axiom of Choice

AC is optimal on some levels (pure algebra),
non-optimal on others (constructive computation).

    Admissible axiom at level k =
    argmin[K(theory_k) + λ_k · coupling violation with L_{k-1}]

There is no "correct" answer.
Different levels use different axiomatics.

### Continuum Hypothesis

NPG(CH or ¬CH) = 0 on all verifiable levels (L0-L8).

No physical, biological, or social prediction changes
depending on CH. Therefore CH has no truth value
in the UAF sense. It has only pragmatic weight.

### Russell's Paradox

Self-reference failure:

    K(model(X) | within X) ≥ K(X)

A complete self-model cannot fit inside the system.
This is the same principle as Gödel, Tarski,
and the impossibility of full self-prediction.

### Infinity

Infinity is the regime where K → 0 for descriptions:

    K(π) = O(1), but K(π truncated to n digits) = O(log n)

The infinite object can be simpler than its finite approximation.

Physical infinity exists only when it is compressible:

    Level ℵ_k realized in system S ⟺
    K(regularity at ℵ_k) ≤ computational budget of S
