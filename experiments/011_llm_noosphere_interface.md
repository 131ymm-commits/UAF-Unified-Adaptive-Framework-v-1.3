Vernadsky's noosphere (1926): the "sphere of reason," humanity's collective cognition enveloping the planet.

- It could accumulate information (books, libraries, universities).

- It had no "nervous system" to integrate disparate knowledge domains.

The Internet (L7→L8 precursor): added connectivity, but not integration.
Data was abundant, but **surprise reduction** across domains remained slow and human-bottlenecked.
