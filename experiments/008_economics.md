# Experiment 008: Economics as Thermodynamics of Prediction

Status: Layer 2 (cross-domain interpretation)

## Problem

Economics relies on "utility" — unmeasurable, unfalsifiable.
Models break constantly (crises, bubbles, irrational behavior).

## UAF Answer

Replace utility with free energy.
Replace preferences with precision-weighted predictions.

### Price

    Price ≡ ΔG (free energy cost of production)

Money is tokenized energy/work.

### Wealth

    Wealth_k = Resources_k - T_k · Uncertainty_k

Analogous to Gibbs free energy: G = H - TS.

### Market equilibrium

Wealth distribution follows Boltzmann-Gibbs:

    n(E) ∝ exp(-E / k_B T_econ)

Inequality is a statistical-mechanical law,
not a moral failing (within closed systems).

### Market crash

Not psychology. Thermodynamic pressure release.

When prediction error spikes (surprise),
free energy rises sharply.
Agents must dump resources to minimize F.
This is a phase transition, not panic.

### Investor behavior

    Action = argmin[Risk + Ambiguity]

When ambiguity exceeds neural tolerance (L4),
fight-or-flight triggers panic selling.
This is hardwired, not irrational.

## Surprise Reduction

Utility function: eliminated.
Crash dynamics: explained as phase transition.
Inequality: derived from statistical mechanics.
All via one equation: minimize free energy.
