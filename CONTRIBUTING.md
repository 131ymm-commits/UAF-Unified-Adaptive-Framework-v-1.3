# Contributing

UAF welcomes criticism.

But criticism must be structured.

---

## Good criticism

A good critique says:

1. Which claim is being challenged.
2. Which layer it belongs to.
3. Which equation or mapping fails.
4. What alternative compresses better.
5. Whether the critique lowers NPG.

---

## Bad criticism

Bad critique says only:

- "This is too broad."
- "This sounds philosophical."
- "This is not how people usually say it."
- "This reminds me of X, therefore it is X."

These may be useful intuitions, but they are not sufficient.

---

## Contribution types

Accepted:

- mathematical corrections,
- formal counterexamples,
- empirical tests,
- implementation improvements,
- domain mappings,
- falsification attempts.

---

## Principle

\[
\text{No authority. No dogma. Predictive gain only.}
\]
